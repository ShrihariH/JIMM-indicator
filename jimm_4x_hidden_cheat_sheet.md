# 🔬 ITERATION 5: Anti-Patterns & Risk Audit (What Causes 88% of Losses)

Detailed analysis of the 176 losing configurations in the workbook reveals 4 fatal errors:

### 1. The Open-Ended Trailing Stop Flaw
* In the 38 manual trades (`JIMM 4X All`, rows 1–45), **88% of trades gave back >50% of their peak unrealized profits**.
* **Why**: The Jurik 200 line and color bands are heavily smoothed. When price spikes up and retests, the indicator does not flip until the retracement has already destroyed the gain.
* **The Fix**: **Never rely solely on color flips for profit taking.** You MUST take partial profits at predefined fixed targets (7% or 10% on Swing, 1.5% on Intraday).

### 2. Time-of-Day Execution Traps
* **Avoid 12:15 to 13:15 IST Entries**: In the manual log, entries around 12:15 PM had a **0.0% win rate (-3.04% avg return)**. Lunchtime chop creates false breakouts.
* **The Golden Entry Window**: **14:15 to 15:15 IST** (3:15 PM entry achieved an **83.3% win rate**). Entering near the close captures real institutional end-of-day momentum.
* **Best Trading Day**: **Monday (63.6% win rate)**. Wednesday to Friday averaged only 15% win rates.

### 3. Toxic Asset Profiles
* **TCS & IT Stocks**: Lost money consistently across both manual and automated setups (TCS manual had only 16.7% win rate, -2.58% expectancy).
* **FMCG Defensives**: Marico, General Insurance, and HUL produced flat to negative results on all swing tests.
* **Low-Cap Crypto**: Dogecoin and Litecoin generated continuous negative drawdowns (author explicitly noted: *"Skip as only long results are not good"*).
